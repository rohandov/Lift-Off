# Privacy Policy for Lift Off

_Last updated: 2026-04-11_

Lift Off ("the app") is a minimal, offline workout tracker developed by Rohan Vora.

## Data we collect

**None.** Lift Off does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All workout data you enter (exercises, sets, reps, history) is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

## Data we share

**None.** The app does not communicate with any servers and does not share data with any third parties.

## Third-party services

Lift Off does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

## Permissions

Lift Off does not request the internet permission or any runtime permissions. It is a fully offline application.

## Children's privacy

Because Lift Off collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
